# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Nodvard Deck to be a friendly place for everyone, regardless of experience, background
or identity. We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, harassment, discriminatory jokes or language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently
ban contributors for behavior they deem harmful.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers by e-mail to
**kontakt@nodvard.com**.
Reports are handled confidentially.
