# Nodvard Deck 0.14.4: fix for Shield

## English

The web interface is in German only for now. Names of buttons, tabs, pages, and settings are given in German as they appear on screen.

### The essentials

A small bugfix release with exactly one change.

- **Shield, quarantine:** Open findings are listed completely again. They were missing when there were more than 200 newer findings that had already been handled. The number on the tab was right, but the list below stayed empty and the findings could not be dealt with.

### What you need to do

Nothing special. If you use the update helper, update as usual with “Jetzt aktualisieren”. Afterwards the open findings are listed under Shield → Quarantäne.

### Thanks

Thanks to the tester from our Discord who reported the bug after a deep scan across several servers.
